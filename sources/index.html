Care for skin folds

Cleveland Clinic, Intertrigo, reviewed August 24, 2021: keeping folds clean and dry, patting rather than rubbing, and getting rashes or signs of infection assessed. NHS, Body odour (BO): washing, drying and changing clothing.

Gentle outside care

NHS, Vaginitis and Vaginal discharge: water for outside care, avoiding douching and scented products, and seeking advice for unusual discharge or persistent symptoms. NHS, Postmenopausal bleeding: any vaginal bleeding after menopause needs evaluation.

Care sources checked November 13, 2026 for the three concern pages. Medical guidance supports the care steps, not endorsements of the pictured products.

Body odor and sweating

  • Cleveland Clinic, Body Odor, medically reviewed March 4, 2022: sweat, skin bacteria, hormone changes, deodorants and antiperspirants.
  • NHS, Body odour (BO), reviewed May 14, 2025: everyday care, pharmacist advice and when to seek assessment.
  • NHS, Vaginal discharge: changes in discharge and symptoms that need advice.
