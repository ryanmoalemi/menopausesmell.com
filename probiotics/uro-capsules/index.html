Why we included it

URO capsules appeared in the earlier research and the linked discussions. People describe positive, negative and uncertain results. That makes the product worth explaining, but does not establish it as the best menopause supplement.

What is in the capsule formula?

The label lists four Lactobacillus species in a 50 mg blend, with 5 billion CFU per serving. CFU means colony-forming units, a measure of live microorganisms. The formula also lists 400 mg of XOS, a prebiotic ingredient.

Read the full label details checked

V-Positiv blend 50 mg, 5 billion CFU total: L. acidophilus, L. rhamnosus, L. reuteri, L. fermentum. XOS 400 mg. Other ingredients maltodextrin, vegetarian cellulose capsule, natural color.

Does it help with menopause-related odor?

Our review did not establish an odor benefit for this exact product in people going through menopause. Reviews describe personal experiences; they do not show that the supplement caused a change.

How is it different from URO gummies?

The gummy uses different bacteria and amounts, plus added vitamins. A capsule review cannot tell you how the gummy will work. See the side-by-side formula comparison.

Before buying

Match the capsule product and serving information to the current label. Check the one-time price against any subscription offer. Ask a clinician about medicines or supplements you already take when relevant. Persistent vaginal odor or other symptoms still need assessment.
