Full ingredient differences

Capsules: four Lactobacillus species in a 50 mg blend, 5 billion CFU, plus 400 mg XOS. Other ingredients include maltodextrin, a vegetarian cellulose capsule and natural color.

Gummies: B. coagulans SNZ 1969 in a 135 mg blend with pineapple and sea-buckthorn powders, 1 billion CFU, vitamin C 45 mg and vitamin D3 50 mcg. Other ingredients include tapioca syrup, sugar, pectin and natural flavor.

Both labels list two units per serving and 30 servings per 60-count bottle. Follow the current label; these facts are for comparison.
