Why It Happens

Menopause can affect temperature regulation, sweating, skin bacteria, and even how smells are perceived.

↘

Estrogen declines

Hormonal shifts can affect temperature regulation and skin physiology.

💧

More sweating

Hot flashes, night sweats, and stress can increase perspiration.

◌

Microbiome shifts

Changes in skin bacteria may create stronger or unfamiliar odors.

≋

Different body odor

Bacteria break down sweat into compounds with a noticeable smell.

◎

Other factors

Stress, diet, hydration, medication, and fabrics may contribute.
