Read the full ingredient list

Water, cocamidopropyl betaine, sodium methyl lauroyl taurate, sodium chloride, glycerin, hydroxystearic acid, stearic acid, cocamide MEA, sodium benzoate, citric acid.

Ingredients can change. Compare the exact product name and the current package, not just a retailer thumbnail. Fragrance-free does not mean irritation is impossible.

What this product does not establish

Ultra Sensitive and ordinary Sensitive Body Wash are different formulas. The label does not establish that either treats menopause-related odor.

This is a label-based comparison. We have not tested the product.
